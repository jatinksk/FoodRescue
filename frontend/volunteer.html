How FoodRescue Works

Volunteers help move surplus food from donors to people and organizations who need it.

Your role as a volunteer

Browse available food, request a pickup, collect the food from the donor location and complete the pickup after collecting it.

1
Find Food

Browse food listings provided by restaurants, canteens, cafes and other donors.

2
Request Pickup

Select a suitable food listing and click Request Pickup to reserve the collection.

3
Collect Food

Visit the donor's pickup location and collect the food that you requested.

4
Complete Pickup

After collecting the food, return to My Pickups and mark the pickup as completed.
